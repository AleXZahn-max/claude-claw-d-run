Claude Claw'd Run

An endless runner rendered as terminal text. On first launch, type a handle for the leaderboard and use the left and right arrows to pick your crab's colour, then press enter. Press space, up arrow or W to jump. Press down arrow or S to duck, or to drop faster while airborne. Collect diamond tokens to charge the extended thinking meter, then press shift to slow the world down. Press L for the leaderboard, C to change your crab, P to pause, T to switch theme, M to mute, F to toggle screen effects.
